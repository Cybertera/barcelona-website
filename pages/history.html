How Barcelona Started

FC Barcelona was founded on November 29, 1899, by a Swiss football enthusiast named Joan Gamper and a group of football players.

The club started as a small football team and gradually became one of the most famous clubs in the world. Barcelona is known for its motto "Més que un club", which means "More than a club".

Important Moments in History

  • 1899 - FC Barcelona was founded.
  • 1929 - Barcelona won the first Spanish League title.
  • 1957 - Camp Nou stadium was opened.
  • 1992 - Barcelona won its first European Cup.
  • 2009 - Barcelona won six major trophies in one year.
  • 2015 - Barcelona won its fifth Champions League title.

Barcelona's Major Achievements

Throughout its history, Barcelona has won many domestic and international competitions. The club has competed against some of the strongest teams in football.
