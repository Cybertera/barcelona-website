About FC Barcelona

Futbol Club Barcelona is one of the most recognized football clubs in the world. Founded in 1899, the club has built a strong identity through its football, history and connection with its supporters.

Our Club Identity

Barcelona is known for its blue and red colours, passionate supporters and commitment to attractive football.
